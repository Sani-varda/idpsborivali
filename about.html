PROPRIETARY EDUCATION MODEL

THE 360° HOLISTIC DEVELOPMENT MODEL

Nurturing every dimension of a child's growth across 6 foundational pillars.

1 • LEARN

Intellectual Thinking & Inquiry

Grounded in CBSE competency benchmarks and NEP 2020, our academic model transitions learners from passive memorization to inquiry-led exploration, scientific deduction, and real-world problem solving.

  • Conceptual Mastery: Rigorous STEM fundamentals connecting textbook theories to daily practical applications.
  • CAIR Coding & AI: Computational logic, algorithmic thinking, and robotics integrated from Grade 1.
  • Inquiry-Based Science: Hands-on experimentation labs encouraging hypotheses, observation, and analysis.
  • Bloom's Taxonomy: Structured learning progression from foundational understanding to high-order synthesis.
Developmental Milestone Cultivates sharp analytical reasoning, scientific temperament, independent research acumen, and academic self-reliance.
2 • EXPLORE

Creative Imagination & Expression

Unlocking lateral thinking, aesthetic sensitivity, and original voice through multi-sensory artistic disciplines, stage performance, and multidimensional design explorations.

  • Visual Arts & Sculpture: Studio pottery, acrylics, textile crafts, and 3D architectural modeling.
  • Performing Arts & Music: Classical Indian rhythms, contemporary Western instrumentation, and vocal mastery.
  • Theatre & Dramatics: Overcoming stage hesitation through role-play, voice projection, and improvisational drama.
  • 100% Stage Opportunity: Inclusive cultural productions guaranteeing every child active stage exposure.
Developmental Milestone Nurtures rich aesthetic appreciation, fearless individuality, divergent creative thinking, and dynamic public confidence.
3 • CREATE

Emotional Well-being & Resilience

Prioritizing mental wellness, emotional intelligence (EQ), and inner balance in a fear-free, joyful environment where every learner feels acknowledged, valued, and emotionally secure.

  • Mindfulness & Pranayama: Daily breathwork circles to enhance focus, calm anxiety, and center attention.
  • In-House Psychologist: Certified counseling support for socio-emotional guidance and developmental milestones.
  • Circle Time Dialogues: Safe peer sharing circles that normalize emotions and build positive self-image.
  • Zero-Bullying Ecosystem: Warm mentor-student relationships based on restorative empathy and mutual trust.
Developmental Milestone Builds high emotional quotient (EQ), self-regulation under pressure, deep personal empathy, and unshakeable resilience.
4 • GROW

Social Leadership & Teamwork

Molding principled leaders who collaborate effortlessly. Through student governance, civic dialogues, and team initiatives, students learn to guide, listen, and achieve shared goals.

  • Democratic Student Council: Authentic elections, house captaincies, and student-led campus committees.
  • Mock Parliament & Debates: Parliamentary simulation forums honing rhetoric, rebuttal, and consensus building.
  • Collaborative Research: Interdisciplinary group missions balancing team leadership and accountability.
  • Etiquette & Financial Literacy: Diplomatic social decorum, public address, and practical financial management.
Developmental Milestone Instills diplomatic negotiation skills, inclusive teamwork, civic maturity, and inspiring group leadership.
5 • LEAD

Physical Fitness & Athleticism

Fostering vitality, neuromuscular coordination, and team sportsmanship. With international-standard facilities and NIS-trained coaches, students embrace enduring wellness habits.

  • Multi-Sport FIFA Turf: All-weather arena for football, basketball, athletics, and active outdoor games.
  • Cricket Practice Nets: Caged turf training lanes with bowling mechanics and technique conditioning.
  • Gymnastics & Motor Skills: Indoor balance studios for early posture correction, flexibility, and agility.
  • Physical Fitness Index: Longitudinal stamina tracking, ergonomic habits, and nutritionist-led diet guidance.
Developmental Milestone Enhances cardiovascular stamina, motor agility, physical self-discipline, and enduring sportsman spirit.
6 • BELONG

Ethical Values & Character Building

Rooting every child in timeless Indian heritage, moral integrity, universal human values, and ecological responsibility to shape conscientious citizens who lead with empathy.

  • Timeless Indian Ethos: Storytelling traditions imparting universal values of Satya, Dharma, and Seva.
  • Environmental Stewardship: "Green Warrior" campus initiatives, waste reduction, and organic planting.
  • Community Outreach (Seva): Intergenerational elder home engagements, donation drives, and civic support.
  • Global Citizenship: Appreciation for cultural diversity, human rights, and constitutional patriotism.
Developmental Milestone Instills uncompromising moral integrity, cultural pride, environmental consciousness, and selfless social service.

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

"Joyful Discovery, Academic Rigor & Lifelong Character Building"

"Welcome to International Delhi Public School, Borivali. We perceive each student not as a vessel to be filled, but as a spark to be ignited. Our everyday mission is to create a dynamic, safe, and child-centered sanctuary where curiosity is celebrated and potential is realized."

Education at IDPS Borivali is aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and the CBSE curriculum.
