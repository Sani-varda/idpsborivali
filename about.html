OUR IDENTITY

Curious Minds. Confident Hearts. Brighter Tomorrows.

International Delhi Public School (IDPS), a unit of Shri Hari Education Trust, in collaboration with St. Rock's High School, brings a progressive CBSE educational experience to Borivali (Gorai-2).

With a strong legacy in education and years of trust, we are committed to nurturing confident, compassionate and responsible future citizens through a blend of academic excellence, modern pedagogy and value-based learning.

  • Inquiry-Based Scientific Learning

2 • EXPLORE

Creative Imagination & Expression

Inspiring out-of-the-box ideas through visual arts, performing theatre, music, creative writing, and multidimensional exploration.

  • Performing Arts & Music Studio
  • Visual Crafts & Design Thinking
  • Creative Storytelling & Theatre
3 • CREATE

Emotional Well-being & Confidence

Nurturing emotional intelligence (EQ), positive self-image, resilience, mental wellness, and daily mindfulness in a caring ecosystem.

  • Daily Mindfulness & Meditation
  • Emotional Resilience & Self-Worth
  • Supportive Counselor & Mentorship
4 • GROW

Social Leadership & Teamwork

Building collaborative leaders through student parliament, group projects, team sports, inter-house debates, and peer empathy.

  • Student Council & Leadership Forums

Physical Fitness & Healthy Living

Instilling active lifestyles through dedicated turf sports, gymnastics, yoga, motor skill development, and balanced wellness habits.

  • Multi-Sport Turf & Play Area

  • Motor Skills & Nutritional Habits

Ethical Values & Character Building

Rooting every child in timeless Indian culture, respect, environmental sensitivity, community service, and global citizenship.

CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

"Empowering Young Minds with Wisdom, Character & Global Competence"

"At IDPS Borivali, in collaboration with St. Rock's High School, we believe true education is a transformative journey that extends far beyond textbooks and examinations. We are committed to nurturing confident, compassionate, and resilient young achievers who will lead India and the world into an enlightened future."

When we established the Gorai-2 Borivali campus under Shri Hari Education Trust, our mandate was uncompromising: to build a premier institution where rigorous CBSE academics converge with digital smart classrooms, AI & Robotics labs, performing arts, sports training, and sound ethical grounding.

We have cultivated a vibrant ecosystem where curiosity is celebrated and every student is encouraged to explore their innate potential. We warmly invite parents to become active partners in this educational journey as we raise tomorrow's empathetic leaders together.

DIRECTOR'S VISION & REAL-LIFE EXPOSURE

PRINCIPAL'S MESSAGE

"Joyful Discovery, Academic Rigor & Lifelong Character Building"

"Welcome to International Delhi Public School, Borivali. We perceive each student not as a vessel to be filled, but as a spark to be ignited. Our everyday mission is to create a dynamic, safe, and child-centered sanctuary where curiosity is celebrated and potential is realized."

Education at IDPS Borivali is aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and the CBSE curriculum. We harmonize intellectual inquiry with experiential learning — empowering our students through hands-on STEM experimentation, computational thinking, language mastery, environmental consciousness, and creative self-expression.

Equally vital is our focus on emotional wellness, collaborative sportsmanship on our campus turf, and social responsibility. Together with our dedicated team of educators, we foster strong partnerships with parents to ensure that every student feels valued, heard, and inspired to reach their highest aspirations.

Inquiry-Based Pedagogy Fostering conceptual mastery over rote learning
Holistic Well-Being Nurturing emotional resilience & physical agility
Parent Partnership Open, continuous collaboration with families
