FROM THE PRINCIPAL'S DESK

"Joyful Discovery, Academic Rigor & Lifelong Character Building"

"Welcome to International Delhi Public School, Borivali. We perceive each student not as a vessel to be filled, but as a spark to be ignited. Our everyday mission is to create a dynamic, safe, and child-centered sanctuary where curiosity is celebrated and potential is realized."

Dear Students, Parents, and Esteemed Well-Wishers,

It is my distinct privilege to welcome you to International Delhi Public School (IDPS Borivali). As educators in a rapidly evolving 21st century, our responsibility extends far beyond delivering a textbook curriculum. We are tasked with shaping inquisitive minds, compassionate hearts, and confident individuals who have the fortitude to question, invent, and lead with empathy.

At IDPS Borivali, our educational framework is rooted in the proven academic excellence of the CBSE curriculum while actively integrating the forward-thinking ethos of the National Education Policy (NEP 2020). We have crafted our classrooms as vibrant collaborative learning labs equipped with interactive digital smart boards, AI and robotics facilities, and dedicated experiential science centers where children learn through discovery rather than passive memorization.

Balancing Academic Rigor with Whole-Child Wellness

True intellectual strength flourishes only when accompanied by emotional wellness and physical vitality. Our expansive campus features professional sports turf for football, basketball, cricket, karate, and yoga, alongside dedicated academies for classical and modern performing arts. Every student is given the opportunity to identify their unique passions and cultivate them under expert mentors, ensuring sound body, calm mind, and sharp intellect.

A Safe, Nurturing & Inclusive Sanctuary

Our campus is designed to be a protective haven where every student is seen, respected, and heard. With 100% CCTV coverage, verified pastoral caregivers, structured anti-bullying initiatives, and proactive counselling support, parents can rest assured that their child's safety and emotional health remain our top priority every single hour of the school day.

Fostering Strong Parent-School Collaboration

Above all, we cherish our partnership with parents. A child's educational journey is most fruitful when home and school walk hand in hand. With open communication channels, regular interactive sessions, transparent feedback, and a shared commitment to values, we ensure that every student at IDPS Borivali feels safe, inspired, and deeply valued.

Our Four Pillars of Academic Excellence

The methodological framework guiding every classroom, laboratory, and activity at IDPS Borivali.

Inquiry-Based Pedagogy

Moving away from rote learning toward deep conceptual mastery, analytical reasoning, and independent problem-solving.

Whole-Child Development

Balancing academic rigor with multi-sport athletic turf, creative fine arts, Indian and western music, and daily mindfulness.

Parent-School Synergy

Transparent communication, regular interactive forums, and continuous collaborative mentoring to support every child's growth.
