Campus Facilities & Infrastructure

State-of-the-art infrastructure designed to nurture academic brilliance, athletic strength, and scientific discovery.

INSPIRING ENVIRONMENT

World-Class Amenities at IDPS Borivali

Every corner of IDPS Borivali is thoughtfully engineered for student comfort, safety, and immersive learning.

Air-Conditioned Smart Classrooms

Equipped with 75-inch 4K interactive touch panels, ergonomic student desks, and climate-controlled ambient comfort.

STEM & AI Robotics Hub

Hands-on micro-controllers, Arduino kits, 3D printing, and coding curriculum preparing students for future tech careers.

Composite Science Laboratories

Dedicated, fully equipped Physics, Chemistry, and Biology lab stations adhering strictly to CBSE safety standards.

Sports Arena & Football Turf

All-weather multi-sports turf for football, basketball, cricket nets, athletic track, and morning yoga sessions.

Music, Dance & Fine Arts Studio

Acoustically treated studio for keyboard, drums, vocal training, classical Indian dance, contemporary choreography, and drama.

Digital Library & Knowledge Center

Extensive library holding 5,000+ fiction and reference books, international periodicals, Kindle e-readers, and reading pods.

GPS-Tracked Safe School Buses

Fleet of speed-governed school buses with live GPS tracking, CCTV, first-aid, and female attendants across Western Mumbai.

24/7 CCTV & Campus Security

Comprehensive electronic surveillance, gated access control, visitor management, and full-time trained security guards.

Infirmary & Wellness Centre

On-campus medical care with a certified full-time nurse, emergency beds, routine health screenings, and hospital tie-ups.
