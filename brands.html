Curriculum Tailored to Each Formative Stage

Admissions open from Nursery onwards up to 5th Standard, structured strictly as per CBSE benchmarks and the National Curriculum Framework.

NEP FOUNDATIONAL STAGE (AGES 3 – 8)

Pre-Primary & Early Primary: Playgroup, Nursery, Jr. KG, Sr. KG, Grades 1 & 2

The early years are when 85% of brain development occurs. At IDPS Borivali, our foundational wing provides a joyful, safe, and sensorial world inspired by experiential playway, Reggio-Emilia, and Montessori methodologies. Young minds acquire linguistic agility, social empathy, and natural curiosity through creative storytelling, musical rhythms, and guided discovery.

Phonics & Early Literacy: Jolly Phonics reading, rich vocabulary building, and creative puppet storytelling.
Experiential Numeracy: Hands-on counting beads, pattern puzzles, and concrete mathematical concept play.
Sensory & Motor Skills: Child-safe play arena, indoor splash pool, tactile sand art, and gross motor agility.
Nurturing Care & Safety: High teacher-student care ratio, dedicated female attendants, and CCTV secured campus.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Academic Questions

Get quick answers regarding our CBSE syllabus, grade offerings, teaching methodology, and admissions process.

Which board affiliation and curriculum does IDPS Borivali follow?

International Delhi Public School (IDPS) Borivali follows the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) curriculum, seamlessly aligned with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020) and National Curriculum Framework for School Education (NCF-SE 2023). Our pedagogy emphasizes concept mastery and experiential inquiry rather than rote learning.

Which classes are open for admission for the 2027–28 academic session?

Admissions are open from Nursery onwards up to 5th Standard. This covers Playgroup, Nursery, Junior KG, Senior KG, and Grades 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5. With limited seats per class, admissions are granted on a first-come, first-served basis following an interactive campus walkthrough.

What makes the 360° Holistic Development Model unique?

Our proprietary 360° Holistic Model ensures your child develops evenly across six foundational dimensions: Intellectual (CBSE concepts & STEM), Creative (Classical Mudra dance & music), Emotional (mindfulness & resilience), Social (Etiquette & Financial Literacy Lab), Physical (cricket turf & gymnastics), and Ethical (moral character and global citizenship).

What languages are included in the curriculum?

English is the primary medium of instruction. As per NEP 2020 multilingual guidelines, students are also taught Hindi and regional language Marathi to foster strong cultural communication and multi-linguistic cognitive fluency.

How can parents apply or book a campus walkthrough?

Parents can submit an inquiry through our Admissions page or call our admissions team directly at +91 77381 96931 or +91 77381 96931. You can also visit our campus in person at RSC 15, Gorai-2, Borivali (West), Mumbai - 400 091.
