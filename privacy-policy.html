LEGAL & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

How International Delhi Public School (IDPS) Borivali collects, uses, and protects parent, guardian, and student information.

Effective Date: November 25, 2026  |  Institution: International Delhi Public School (IDPS) Borivali (A unit of Shri Hari Education Trust, in collaboration with St. Rock's High School)  |  Website: https://idpsborivali.com

International Delhi Public School, Borivali ("IDPS Borivali", "we", "our", or "the School"), operated under Shri Hari Education Trust in collaboration with St. Rock's High School, respects the privacy of parents, legal guardians, students, prospective applicants, and website visitors. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, process, store, and safeguard personal information when you visit idpsborivali.com or submit an admission or contact inquiry, in accordance with applicable Indian laws including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us or that is automatically generated during normal website operation:

  • Admission & Contact Inquiry Details: Parent or guardian full name, student name and age, grade seeking admission (Nursery to 7th Standard), mobile phone number, email address, inquiry category, subject, and message content submitted through forms on our Home, Admissions, or Contact pages.
  • Application Status Lookup Data: Application reference number and registered mobile number entered into the Admission Application Status widget.
  • Communication & Outreach Records: Details shared when you contact our admissions office by phone, email (admissions@idpsborivali.com or info@idpsborivali.com), or WhatsApp (+91 77381 96931).
  • Technical, Analytics & Attribution Data: Browser/device metadata, landing page, referring URL, campaign parameters (such as utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, and gclid), and interaction telemetry (such as clicks on call or WhatsApp buttons).

2. Children's & Student Privacy

Our website and admission inquiry forms are intended to be used by parents and legal guardians on behalf of prospective students (Nursery to 7th Standard). We do not knowingly allow minors to submit personal data independently through this website without parental or guardian involvement. Any student information (such as child's name, age, and target grade) provided by a parent or guardian is used strictly for admission counseling, scheduling parent-student interactions, and academic enrollment administration.

3. How We Use Your Information

Personal information collected through our website is used solely for legitimate educational and administrative purposes:

  • Responding to admission inquiries, campus tour requests, career inquiries, and parent support messages.
  • Scheduling and coordinating Parent & Student Interactions with our academic and admissions counselors.
  • Allowing applicants and parents to verify the live stage and status of an existing admission inquiry.
  • Communicating important school admission updates, academic calendars, and fee/enrollment guidance via phone, SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
  • Evaluating website performance, improving user experience, and measuring the effectiveness of our outreach campaigns.
  • Preventing automated spam, fraudulent submissions, and abuse of our web forms.

4. CRM Processing, Analytics & Third-Party Services

To operate our website and admissions workflow effectively, we use trusted service providers and tools:

  • Admissions CRM (Sensora CRM): Inquiries submitted through our web forms and status lookups are securely transmitted over HTTPS to our school CRM API (api.idpsborivali.com) so our admissions team can track and respond to your request promptly.
  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) & Google Tag Manager (GTM): We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregated website traffic, page visits, and visitor engagement. Google may set cookies or collect anonymized/pseudonymized usage identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Session Storage & Essential Cookies: Our site uses browser sessionStorage to remember your initial landing page during a browsing session for accurate inquiry attribution, along with essential cookies or tokens used by security/CDN providers (such as Cloudflare and Vercel) to keep the site fast and protected.
  • External Links & Embedded Maps: Our pages contain links to WhatsApp, Facebook, Instagram, and an embedded Google Map. Interacting with those third-party services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

We do not sell, rent, or trade parent or student personal data to third-party marketers or data brokers.

5. Data Security & Retention

All data transmitted between your browser, our website, and our CRM endpoints is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Access to inquiry records is restricted to authorized IDPS Borivali admissions and administrative personnel. We retain inquiry and application records only for as long as necessary to fulfill admission processing, academic administration, or legal and regulatory obligations.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable law, parents and visitors may:

  • Request access to or a summary of the personal information you submitted to us.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate contact or student details.
  • Withdraw consent or request deletion of your inquiry record (subject to any statutory or school record-keeping requirements).
  • Opt out of promotional or admission follow-up messages at any time by contacting us or replying to our communications.
  • Disable or clear browser cookies and sessionStorage via your browser settings.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our admissions processes, website features, or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.

8. Contact & Grievance Redressal

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, wish to exercise your data rights, or have a privacy concern, please contact our Campus Office:
