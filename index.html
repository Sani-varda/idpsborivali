ABOUT US

Curious Minds. Confident Hearts. Brighter Tomorrows.

International Delhi Public School (IDPS), a unit of Shri Hari Education Trust, in collaboration with St. Rock's High School, brings a progressive CBSE educational experience to Borivali (Gorai-2).

With a strong legacy in education and years of trust, we are committed to nurturing confident, compassionate and responsible future citizens through a blend of academic excellence, modern pedagogy and value-based learning.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Parent Admission FAQ: IDPS Borivali

Everything you need to know about our admissions, curriculum, safety, and school life.

Which grades are currently open for admission at IDPS Borivali?

Admissions are officially open for Academic Year 2027–28 for Nursery to 7th Standard (with personalized Parent-Student interaction) at our Borivali (Gorai-2) campus. Seats are allocated on a first-come, first-evaluated basis due to limited intake per batch.

What is the 360° Holistic Development model at IDPS?

Our proprietary 360° Holistic Development Model is built upon 6 vibrant rainbow dimensions: 1. Intellectual Thinking & Problem Solving (LEARN), 2. Creative Imagination & Expression (EXPLORE), 3. Emotional Well-being & Confidence (CREATE), 4. Social Leadership & Teamwork (GROW), 5. Physical Fitness & Healthy Living (LEAD), and 6. Ethical Values & Responsible Citizenship (BELONG).

What safety and security arrangements exist on campus?

IDPS Borivali maintains a 100% secure campus with 24/7 CCTV surveillance, biometric visitor logging, female security guards and nannies, touchless sanitized restrooms, and GPS-tracked school buses with real-time route alerts for parents.

How can parents schedule a personalized campus tour?

Parents can schedule a campus walkthrough by filling out our online admission inquiry form or contacting our admissions office directly at +91 77381 96931 or admissions@idpsborivali.com.
