CHAIRMAN'S MESSAGE

"True education is a transformative journey that equips students to navigate and lead in a complex, fast-evolving world."

At IDPS Borivali, we believe that education must transcend textbooks and examination scores. Our sacred mission is to cultivate compassionate leaders, creative problem-solvers, and ethically-grounded citizens who stand tall on global platforms while remaining deeply anchored in Indian values.

Dear Parents, Students, and Esteemed Well-Wishers,

It gives me immense pride and joy to welcome you to International Delhi Public School (IDPS), Borivali. When we laid the cornerstone of the International Delhi Public Schools Society, our driving aspiration was simple yet profound: to make transformative, world-class education accessible to ambitious young minds across the length and breadth of India.

Today, with a pan-India network of over 345+ CBSE-affiliated institutions across 25 states, IDPS has grown into one of India’s most trusted educational movements. Every campus under our umbrella represents an ecosystem of inquiry, innovation, and intellectual rigour, guided by our sacred motto: ‘Service for Nation’.

Our Borivali campus stands as a beacon of this vision in Mumbai. Here, we have thoughtfully blended rigorous CBSE academics with experiential STEM curricula, cutting-edge robotics and computer laboratories, expansive athletic infrastructure, and creative arts studios. We do not just teach children to memorise concepts; we teach them to think critically, question fearlessly, and act compassionately.

Education is a collaborative partnership among teachers, parents, and the institution. As we embark upon each new academic year, my assurance to every parent who entrusts us with their child is that IDPS Borivali will nurture their individuality, protect their curiosity, and empower them to achieve their highest potential.
