TRANSPARENT & SIMPLE ENROLLMENT

How to Enroll Your Child at IDPS Borivali

We ensure a smooth, transparent, and parent-friendly admission process focused on understanding your child's unique capabilities.

1

Step 1: Enquiry

Submit the online enquiry form or connect directly with our admissions helpline at +91 77381 96931 for classes from Nursery to 7th Standard.

2

Step 2: Campus Tour

Visit our state-of-the-art Borivali campus at Gorai-2. Experience digital smart classrooms, AI & Robotics labs, performing arts studios, and athletic turf.

3

Step 3: Interaction with Parents & Students

A warm, collaborative dialogue: A personal and informal interaction between our educators, parents, and the student. We discover your child's curiosities, hobbies, and individual learning style without stressful written tests.

4

Step 4: Enrollment

Submit required documents and complete enrollment formalities to confirm your child's seat!

ENROLLMENT CHECKLIST

Documents Required for Admission

Please prepare the following basic documents at the time of your interaction with our academic team:

  • Original Birth Certificate (For Pre-Primary & Grade 1)
  • Previous School Transfer Certificate (TC) & Progress Report Card
  • Student's Aadhaar Card Copy
  • 4 Recent Passport-size photographs of the student
  • Parents' Aadhaar Cards & Residential Address Proof
  • Student Medical Fitness Record & Blood Group certificate
