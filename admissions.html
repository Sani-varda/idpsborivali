SIMPLE 4-STEP PROCESS

How to Enroll at IDPS Borivali

We ensure a smooth, transparent, and parent-friendly admission process for every family.

Step 1: Enquiry

Fill out the online admission enquiry or call our admissions helpline at +91 77381 96931.

Step 2: Campus Tour

Visit our modern Borivali campus, explore smart classrooms, STEM labs, and sports facilities.

Step 3: Interaction

A friendly, informal interaction with our academic coordinator to understand your child's needs.

Step 4: Enrollment

Submit required documents, complete admission formalities, and welcome your child to IDPS Borivali!

CHECKLIST

Documents Required for Admission

  • Original Birth Certificate (Pre-Primary & Grade 1)
  • Previous School Transfer Certificate (TC) & Report Card
  • Student's Aadhaar Card Copy
  • 4 Passport-size photographs of the student
  • Parents' Aadhaar Card & Address Proof
  • Medical Fitness Certificate & Blood Group record
