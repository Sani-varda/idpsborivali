DIRECTOR'S VISION & PHILOSOPHY

"Education must move away from the four walls of rote memorization towards real-time experience and authentic real-life exposure."

"When a child is only asked to memorize answers for an examination, learning ends with the bell. But when a child rolls up their sleeves, conducts an experiment, argues a moot court case, prototypes a working robotic model, and manages a mock financial budget, knowledge becomes an unshakeable life skill."
— Meera Divyesh Desai, Director, IDPS Borivali

Dear Parents, Guardians, and Visionary Educators,

Welcome to International Delhi Public School (IDPS), Borivali. As the Director of this institution under the aegis of Shri Hari Education Trust, I have often asked a fundamental question that shapes everything we do here: What is the true purpose of school in the twenty-first century?

For decades, traditional Indian schooling has been trapped in a rote-memorization cycle—where children are taught to commit textbook paragraphs to memory, reproduce them onto question papers, and receive grades that reflect rote retention rather than practical capability. Yet, the moment our young graduates step out into the real world, they are confronted with challenges that textbooks never scripted: navigating rapid technological disruptions, leading diverse teams with empathy, resolving complex ethical dilemmas, and solving unstructured problems in real time.

The Shift: Why Real-Time Experience-Based Learning Matters

At IDPS Borivali, we have decisively moved away from the passive "chalk-and-talk" paradigm. We believe that the human brain learns best by doing, experiencing, and reflecting. In our classrooms, science is not read off a chalkboard—it is discovered by formulating hypotheses in modern scientific laboratories. Mathematics is not an abstract chore—it is applied through architectural geometry, budgeting simulations, and data modeling. Language is not merely grammar exercises—it is honed on public podiums, theatrical stages, and editorial desks.

Real-time experience means that our students do not wait until university or employment to touch reality. From the foundational years in Kindergarten through Grade 10, every child is invited to be an active creator, investigator, and decision-maker.

Uncompromising Commitment to Real-Life Exposure

A child cannot learn swimming by reading a manual on hydrodynamics; they must enter the water. In the same way, children cannot learn leadership, civic duty, financial resilience, or empathy through abstract definitions. That is why our curriculum at IDPS Borivali is deeply anchored in Real-Life Exposure:

  • The Ashoka Moot Court Experience: Students step into our dedicated judicial court chamber to argue constitutional dilemmas, study case law, and internalize justice, civic integrity, and public debate with evidence.
  • Financial & Practical Life Etiquette: Students are introduced to age-appropriate financial literacy—budgeting, savings, digital transactions, and smart entrepreneurship—alongside formal dining etiquette and social grace.
  • Hands-On Maker & STEM Labs: Students construct real working circuits, prototype robotics, program code, and study environmental sensors to solve tangible problems within their community.
  • Community & Environmental Immersion: Regular field visits, organic kitchen gardens, cleanliness drives, and social collaborations instill genuine compassion and ecological consciousness.
  • Emotional Intelligence & Rainbow Mindfulness: Through dedicated yoga and mindfulness spaces, we teach children how to regulate stress, navigate peer conflict, and cultivate internal tranquility.

My Personal Pledge to Every Parent

When you entrust your child to IDPS Borivali, you are not merely enrolling them in a school; you are initiating them into an empowering journey of self-discovery, intellectual boldness, and joyful inquiry. We promise to cherish their individual spark, challenge them to think deeply, give them the courage to fail and learn, and ensure they emerge as self-assured, compassionate, and globally competitive leaders.

I warmly invite you to visit our campus, observe our interactive learning studios in motion, and experience firsthand how real-time education can transform your child’s future.

Meera Divyesh Desai
